RESEARCH OUTPUT · 2026

Publications & preprints

Six co-authored research preprints (2026), submitted to IEEE ICRA 2027. Research materials and demonstrations: Rosota Intelligence Institute.

  1. From Instrument-Mounted Demonstrations to In-Vivo Execution: Learning Bimanual Laparoscopic Appendectomy Without Robot-Collected Demonstrations
    Dongho Yee, Juahn Oh, Jinseok Lee, Jiyul Lee, Yechan Seo, Seong Jeong, Minsung Kim, Seonho Shim, Younghoon Noh, Hyuk Choi, Youngbin Kong, Kyu Eun Lee, and Hyoun-Joong Kong
    arXiv preprint (2026). Submitted to IEEE ICRA 2027.
    An end-to-end pipeline for learning bimanual surgical policies from hand-held instrument demonstrations, with simulation-based policy selection and in-vivo rabbit evaluation.

  2. Recording Hand-Held Laparoscopic Instrument Motion in the Operating Room: Magnetometer-Free Fusion of Inertial, Range and Visual Sensing
    Jiyul Lee, Dongho Yee, Juahn Oh, Jinseok Lee, Yechan Seo, Seong Jeong, Minsung Kim, Seonho Shim, Younghoon Noh, Hyuk Choi, Youngbin Kong, and Hyoun-Joong Kong
    arXiv preprint (2026). Submitted to IEEE ICRA 2027.
    A clip-on instrument logger combining inertial, range, and visual sensing to recover laparoscopic instrument motion without magnetic measurements.

  3. Contact-Stable Deformable Tissue Simulation Using Implicit Integration and Live-Pose Grasp Constraints for Laparoscopic Surgery Robot Policy Evaluation
    Juahn Oh, Dongho Yee, Jinseok Lee, Jiyul Lee, Yechan Seo, Seong Jeong, Minsung Kim, Seonho Shim, Younghoon Noh, Hyuk Choi, Youngbin Kong, and Hyoun-Joong Kong
    arXiv preprint (2026). Submitted to IEEE ICRA 2027.
    A reconstructed deformable-tissue simulation environment with stable contact and grasp constraints for closed-loop surgical robot policy screening.

  4. Surgical Master Console Using General-Purpose Robot Arms and a Separable Articulated Distal Interface: Porcine In-Vivo Evaluation
    Minsung Kim, Seonho Shim, Dongho Yee, Younghoon Noh, Juahn Oh, Yechan Seo, Jinseok Lee, Jiyul Lee, Seong Jeong, Hyuk Choi, Youngbin Kong, and Hyoun-Joong Kong
    arXiv preprint (2026). Submitted to IEEE ICRA 2027.
    A bimanual surgical master console built from general-purpose robot arms and separable wrist-and-grasp interfaces, evaluated in a porcine procedure.

  5. Contact-Adaptive Robotic Ultrasound Probe Control for Tissue Exploration and Continuous Task-Relevant Visualization Using Robot-Free Image-Motion Demonstration
    Seong Jeong, Minsung Kim, Dongho Yee, Juahn Oh, Yechan Seo, Jiyul Lee, Jinseok Lee, Seonho Shim, Younghoon Noh, Youngbin Kong, and Hyoun-Joong Kong
    arXiv preprint (2026). Submitted to IEEE ICRA 2027.
    Demonstration-informed ultrasound probe control with force regulation to maintain a clinician-selected view, evaluated on a dynamic bladder phantom.

  6. Drape-Compatible Tool-Tip Localization for Hand-Held Laparoscopic Instruments via UWB Carrier-Phase Ranging and Trocar-Constrained Geometry
    Jinseok Lee, Dongho Yee, Minsung Kim, Younghoon Noh, Seonho Shim, Juahn Oh, Yechan Seo, Jiyul Lee, Seong Jeong, Hyuk Choi, and Hyoun-Joong Kong
    arXiv preprint (2026). Submitted to IEEE ICRA 2027.
    Tool-tip localization using UWB carrier-phase ranging, inertial sensing, and trocar constraints, designed to operate with sterile drapes.

Research

  • (1) AI Research
    • Co-First Author: Machine learning-based models for optimizing outpatient follow-up schedules after cervical spinal laminoplasty surgery
      Published in BMC Medical Informatics and Decision Making (2024)
      https://doi.org/10.1186/s12911-024-02693-y
      Research Summary: Developed and validated a machine learning-based framework to optimize clinical follow-up schedules for patients undergoing cervical laminoplasty. Leveraged time-series patient data, including the Japanese Orthopedic Association (JOA) scores from multiple postoperative timepoints, to predict 2-year outcomes using various machine learning algorithms. Achieved the highest predictive performance (AUC 0.90 ± 0.13) with an LSTM-based model.
  • (2) AI + BCI Research
    • First Author: Functional Near-Infrared Spectroscopy Signals as Biomarkers for the Quantitative Assessment of Nausea: A Pilot Study
      Presented at the Spring Academic Conference of The Korean Society of Medical Informatics (KOSMI) (2024)
      Research Summary: Conducted a pilot study exploring the use of functional Near-Infrared Spectroscopy (fNIRS) signals as biomarkers for objectively quantifying nausea, a primary symptom of cybersickness. Achieved a significant predictive accuracy with an R² value of 0.9045.
    • First Author: Development of an EEG-Based Real-Time Attention State Classification Machine Learning Model: Enhancing Achievement in Medical VR Training
      Presented at the Korean Society of Computer Information (KSCI) Spring Academic Conference (2024)
      Award: Best Paper Award
      Research Summary: Developed a real-time EEG-based attention state classification model integrated into a VR training program for preoperative education, achieving a high performance (AUROC = 0.9336).
  • (3) BCI
    • First Author: Generating a Realistic Rolling Illusion in Immersive Virtual Reality Environment Using Galvanic Vestibular Stimulation
      Presented at the Fall Academic Conference of The Korean Society of Medical Informatics (KOSMI) (2024)
      Research Summary: Investigated the efficacy of transcranial alternating current stimulation (tACS) applied to the vestibular nerve for inducing a rolling illusion in a VR environment, enhancing the sense of presence.
